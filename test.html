Features

Fro-vy is designed to give you instant confidence in the products you use every day. From personalised ingredient filtering to multilingual explanations, every feature is built to make safety effortless. Explore the tools that help you make informed choices.

How Fro-vy Works

Fro-vy is built to make everyday product checking effortless. Whether you're checking food or skincare products, Fro-vy guides you through a clear four-step process to ensure the ingredients you consume or apply are safe for your allergies, conditions, and health needs.

  • Create Your Profile

    Sign up and tell Fro-vy about your allergies, medical conditions.

  • Choose a Method

    Select the most convenient way to check a product

  • Fro-vy Analyzes Ingredients

    Fro-vy compares every scanned or entered ingredient against your saved allergies, intolerances, and conditions.

  • Get Instant Warnings

    Receive clear, colour-coded alerts showing which ingredients are unsafe for you.
