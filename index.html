Features

Personalised Ingredient Screening

Fro-vy evaluates every ingredient based on your allergies, intolerances, medications, and health conditions, ensuring safety recommendations tailored uniquely to you.

Instant Ingredient Translation & Explanation

Complex ingredient names are automatically translated into Sinhala or Tamil with simple, science-backed explanations so you always understand what you’re consuming or applying.

Unified Food & Skincare Analysis

No need for multiple apps—Fro-vy checks both food and skincare ingredients in one streamlined system, giving you complete product safety in a single place.

Multiple Ways to Check Ingredients

Fro-vy allows users to analyze product ingredients through three convenient methods: OCR label scanning for real-time extraction from packaging Manual ingredient entry for custom lists Product database search for popular stored items All three methods lead to the same goal—accurate safety analysis based on your health profile.

Comprehensive Ingredient Knowledge Base

Fro-vy is supported by a broad scientific database of allergens, harmful compounds, medical triggers, and cosmetic irritants—ensuring accurate and reliable results every time.

Clear, User-Friendly Alerts

The interface highlights risk levels using simple, easy-to-read alerts, helping you quickly identify allergens or medical risks based on your stored conditions and medications.

How Fro-vy Works

Fro-vy is built to make everyday product checking effortless. Whether you're checking food or skincare products, Fro-vy guides you through a clear four-step process to ensure the ingredients you consume or apply are safe for your allergies, conditions, and health needs.

  • Create Your Profile

    Sign up and tell Fro-vy about your allergies, medical conditions.

  • Choose a Method

    Select the most convenient way to check a product

  • Fro-vy Analyzes Ingredients

    Fro-vy compares every scanned or entered ingredient against your saved allergies, intolerances, and conditions.

  • Get Instant Warnings

    Receive clear, colour-coded alerts showing which ingredients are unsafe for you.
